2. **(a)** Commit each run with its paradata file. Check that `config.prompt.category_case_folding` is `true` and the `config.prompt` sha is the same across runs, unless a variant is intended.
   - Correct `llm_config.txt:48`: six vLLM runs are done.
   - Where the run directories may live depends on step 3.
3. **(a)** Before committing, count the categories per run as the digest's table does. `Artemísion`, `Slovensko` and the like on lines that are not about them mean the fix was not in the run.

### D2 — the evaluation data
4. **(c) The home of the corpus** (hub #16). The 16 real documents' text is already public here: the run outputs, `gold_workbook.csv` and `corpus_term_evidence.csv`.
   - **If @motyc approves:** commit `DOC_LINE_CATEG`/`UDP` for the 16, so the sheets are reproducible (S13), and record the approval and licence in `data_samples/LICENSE`/README.
   - **If not:**
     - keep the corpus, the gold labels and the run outputs on the cluster;
     - commit keys-only versions (`file_id,page,line` + labels), a checksum and provenance record of the corpus, and the aggregate scores;
     - remove the text from `test` (history as hub #16 decides).
5. **(c/a) Gold labels.** Annotate `gold_workbook.csv` under the rubric: the annotator picks from `candidate_terms`, including bracketed forms, and meta-text is a category. Who annotates is open; @david-spacil ruled the rubric. A first pass on the 16 real documents (2,156 lines) is the target. A stratified subset of ~500 lines is the fallback if the full set is too costly.
6. **(a) The scorer (S6):** `eval/score_categories.py` over a run directory and the gold sheet. It reports:
   - strict and decay-by-rungs accuracy (D1-a, from `specificity_pairs.csv`);
   - composite pairs either way (D1-b, `same_as`);
   - label-only, except split homonyms resolved from the ids (D1-c/d);
   - per facet and overall; P/R/F1 on `_cs`, `_en` as a consistency check; meta-text, context-for-content (D1-f) and narrower-than-gold in their own confusion cells (D1-e).

   Tests: hand-built gold and runs covering every cell.
7. **(a) A worked example (S4):** 20 real lines scored under strict and decay, side by side, posted with the first score.
8. **(a) Corpus sheets (S7):** `python3 corpus_review.py --all` on the cluster, against the 4,711-term vocabulary; commit only what step 4 allows.

### E — scores and the choice
9. **(b/a)** Score the shortlist and the clean re-runs. Post one table on #2: per model, strict/decay accuracy, per facet, meta-text and D1-f cells, missing lines, seconds per line.
10. **(b/a)** A2-grouping (S14): the best model under `facet_sub`, `facet` and `flat`, on the same lines. This answers @motyc's 08-26 question with a number.
11. **(c)** Choose the model for the service's controlled kind and for the cluster recipe. Record it in `llm_config.txt`, the README and `para_config.txt` (model licence), and on #2.
    - The same Qwen 3.8 deployment can serve atrium-translator#4's LLM arm (AMČR, atrium-project#72).
12. **(a)** P3 evidence: per-facet accuracy and term frequency (S9) say whether Q2 (Related Disciplines & Society) earns its place. A proposal to retire a facet goes to @motyc.

## Done-criteria
- Clean runs with paradata for every scored model.
- A scored evaluation on the real lines, per model and per facet, under the D1 rubric.
- The corpus and gold labels in a place hub #16 allows, and reproducible by someone other than their author (committed, or checksum + provenance).
