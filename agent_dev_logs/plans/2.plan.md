  validated AMČR/TEATER term or is marked unresolved, never forced onto an unrelated term. Equivalent terms are joined

   - **Who:** a Czech-speaking archaeologist; asked of @david-spacil or someone at AMČR (comment on #2). @david-spacil ruled the rubric.

   - **Format, per line:**
     - `line_text`;
     - `is_vocab_term_present`;
     - `term_extracted_1`, empty only when the flag is `False`;
     - `term_extracted_2` and `term_extracted_3`, optional.

     Terms come from the vocabulary; `candidate_terms` stays as a hint.
   - **(a) The sheet:** `gold_workbook.csv` (written by `corpus_review.py`, `gold_workbook_rows`) takes these columns in place of the single `gold_category`, before it goes to the annotator.
6. **(a) The scorer (S6):** `score_categories.py` has the coverage, forced-label, agreement and before/after half (10-19) and strict accuracy where `gold_category` is filled. `gold_labels` moves to the step-5 columns:
   - a pick is correct when it matches any of the one to three gold terms;
   - a line flagged `False` is correct only when no term is given (meta-text, or unresolved where the method allows it).

   With gold, it adds:
   - strict and decay-by-rungs accuracy (D1-a, from `specificity_pairs.csv`);
   - composite pairs either way (D1-b, `same_as`);
   - label-only, except split homonyms resolved from the ids (D1-c/d);
   - per facet and overall; P/R/F1 on `_cs`, `_en` as a consistency check; meta-text, context-for-content (D1-f) and narrower-than-gold in their own confusion cells (D1-e).

   Tests: hand-built gold and runs covering every cell.
7. **(a) A worked example (S4):** 20 real lines scored under strict and decay, side by side, posted with the first score.
8. **(a) Corpus sheets (S7):** `python3 corpus_review.py --all` on the cluster, against the 4,711-term vocabulary; commit only what step 4 allows.

### Option 4 — decision models (11-05)
13. **(a/b)** [`3.plan.md`](3.plan.md), tracked in [#3](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-keyword-extract/issues/3).
    - **What:** a decision model (Clef, Clef-Flash, Strands Decider 2B; `POST /v1/systemone`) answers typed
      questions with a probability per option, and generates no text.
    - **How:** it descends the vocabulary: meta-text, then facet, then group, then down the terms' own tree. The
      tree's parents are the nearest offered ancestors of `specificity_pairs.csv`; it has 797 inner nodes that are
      themselves terms, and up to 11 levels.
      - Each step is a `choice` of at most 255 options plus `<this term>` and `none_of_these`.
      - Only `historické oblasti a státní útvary` (~260 children) needs chunking.
    - **Why:**
      - D2 holds by construction: a term from the list, or unresolved;
      - no prefix trap;
      - the whole vocabulary is reachable at any window (the prompt's 58,260 tokens cut it to ~1,100 terms at 16k);
      - Clef shares Qwen3.8-27B with the default model.
    - **What it does not do:** extract keywords.
    - **When:** runs without gold now, through `score_categories.py` beside R3; with gold, it enters step 9 beside
      the constrained prompt and Option 3.
    - No change to the service, the prompt or the defaults.
