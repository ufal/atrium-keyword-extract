## Context
Keywords are made in two repositories today, by two unrelated methods, and reach the record by neither: nlp-enrich's
statistical keywords go to its HTTP response and its TEITOK header; llm-enrich's controlled terms go to the record's
`enrichment` block. Both repositories also carry a copy of the same LLM engine and vocabulary code, partly diverged —
the one large duplication the report counts (item 5). The meeting cut along the stage instead: **one repository, one
service, all keywords**.

What that buys, beyond the cleanup:
- **#72's hardest item solves itself.** "nlp-enrich drops its LLM stage", "one LLM engine and client" and "one
  versioned vocabulary artefact" become one move rather than a deletion plus a governed copy.
- **nlp-enrich becomes a thin, CPU-only LINDAT client.** `yake`, `keybert` and `sentence-transformers`
  (`requirements.txt:7-9`) take torch with them; the GPU plan (report item 8) keeps KeyBERT on the first GPU, now
  inside keyword-extractor.
- **The `keywords` block gets one owner that also owns `enrichment`**, so discovery reads both keyword kinds from one
  producer, kept apart by block (#73).

## Strategic actions

### A — Create the repository with the history of what it takes

**State 2026-10-01:** ✅ created from nlp-enrich's history (its LLM/vocabulary copy included); llm-enrich's history was not merged, so its copy stays in digital-convert until removed there.
1. **Seed by history, not by copy.** `git filter-repo` a clone of llm-enrich down to the paths that leave it
   (llm-enrich#29 plan §B.2), a clone of nlp-enrich down to `keywords.py`, `kw_config.txt`, `ker_data/` and their tests,
   then merge the two (`--allow-unrelated-histories`) into the new repository. `git log --follow` then works for every
   moved file, which is what reviewers and the trust gate's diff review read.
2. **One copy of the diverged files.** Of the three that differ, **llm-enrich's is the base** (it carries the service
   path and the later fixes: `llm_utils.py` is 101 KB there against 84 KB here); the nlp-enrich-only changes are
   ported one by one from `diff`, each with its test, not merged blindly.
3. **Tag the source states** before cutting: llm-enrich's last LLM release (0.9.0, pending) and nlp-enrich 0.23.0, so
   the new repository's first release notes can name what it was built from.
4. **Shared modules** come by `scripts/revendor_shared.sh` like every tool repository's (hub: add the repository to
   its list, `:69-72`, and to `update_issues.sh` `REPOS`); para-drift then checks them. The record → Markdown renderer
   the LLM path needs (`json_to_md`, `layout_md`, `xml_to_md`, `teitok_read`, `bbox_scale`) is **vendored from the
   converter with SHA pins**, the pattern of llm-enrich's `tests/test_vendored_teitok_parity.py`.
5. **Identity**: `SERVICE = "atrium-keyword-extractor"`, `CITATION.cff` with the authors of both sources, README,
   Pages site, the ARUP-CAS fork, an SSHOMP record (the existing llm-enrich record describes this stage — re-point it),
   the Galaxy wrapper (#66).

### B — The service: `POST /extract_keywords`

**State 2026-10-01:** ✅ statistical kind released (v1.0.0-beta); controlled kind not wired into the service.
| Aspect       | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|--------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Input        | the record after nlp-enrich (`document_json`), or text (`/extract_keywords_text`); the AMČR seed rules of #71 apply                                                                                                           |
| Kinds        | `kind=statistical` · `controlled` · `both` (default `both`); each kind can fail or be skipped on its own and says so                                                                                                          |
| Statistical  | `method=keybert` (default, `DEFAULT_KW_METHOD`), `yake`, `legacy` (KER); `num_keywords`; per document **and per page** (the Digital Archive indexes pages, report §3.1)                                                       |
| Its text     | `lines[].text` for YAKE and KeyBERT, `lines[].lemma`/`upos` for KER (written by nlp-enrich); lines with `categ` Trash or Empty left out (report §5)                                                                           |
| Controlled   | the LLM over the AMČR and TEATER vocabularies, as llm-enrich does it today; `teater_category`, `extracted_keywords_cs/en` and the page citation (`enrichment.items`)                                                          |
| Entity links | `entities[].pid` to AMČR and AAT, as llm-enrich writes them today                                                                                                                                                             |
| Output       | the record with `keywords` (new, #73), `enrichment`, `entities[].pid`; the `CreateAction` (#71 R2)                                                                                                                            |
| Contract     | typed request and response, registered reason codes, limits from the environment and in `/info` (#32, #53, #58); `/info` also reports the methods, the embedding model, the LLM backend and model, and the vocabulary version |

**One service, not two.** The kinds share the input, the record, the vocabulary and the owner; splitting them would
double the images for one stage. Their runtimes differ, which the next point handles.

**Where the LLM runs.** The service is a **client** of an LLM inference server (vLLM or Ollama on the second GPU,
report item 8; OpenRouter for development) through the shared client that llm-enrich already has
(`llm_client_shared.py`). The image carries the sentence-transformers model for KeyBERT and no LLM weights; the
in-process vLLM image (`llm` target today) stays a batch/research target, off the release line. Who operates the
inference server on the cluster is a question for Ronald Harasim (aiscr-docs-pipeline#9).

### C — The record

**State 2026-10-01:** ✅ `keyword-extract` is the successor of `llm-enrich` in the hub's `PROGRAM_SUCCESSORS`; ❌ no `keywords` block (#73).
1. **Successor, not re-attribution.** Following the pilot-baseline proposal (hub, *`doc-schema-v1` as the pilot
   baseline*): `keyword-extractor` is added as a writer beside `llm-enrich` for `enrichment`, `forms` and
   `entities[].pid`, and one alias map (`PROGRAM_SUCCESSORS`) lets every comparison treat old stamps as the successor's.
   Records already written keep `llm-enrich` in `assembled.blocks`, which stays true.
2. **`keywords` is a new block** (additive, #73), owned by `keyword-extractor` from its first day — no re-attribution.
3. **One schema round** with #71's `sha512` and seed profile and #73's quality summary.
4. **TEITOK:** the projection (`api_util/teitok_project.py`, which stays here) reads both blocks and writes
   `@resp="#app-keyword-extractor"` for both kinds, with `@scheme="#kw-<method>"` on statistical terms. It runs as
   nlp-enrich's `/project_record` **after** keyword-extractor, so the chain is `nlp-enrich /enrich` →
   `keyword-extractor /extract_keywords` → `nlp-enrich /project_record` (optional, when TEITOK is wanted).

### D — nlp-enrich after the move: LINDAT only

**State 2026-10-01:** ✅ released as nlp-enrich v1.0.0-beta (a major bump: `kw_method`, `num_keywords`, `keywords`, `method_*`, `llm`, `keyword_methods` removed).
1. **Remove**: `keywords.py`, `kw_config.txt`, `ker_data/`, the `llm` stage (`llm_run.py`, `llm_utils.py`,
   `vocab_*.py`, `prompt_template.py`, `prompts/`, `llm_config.txt`, `corpus_review.py`, `requirements_llm.txt`), the
   stages `keywords` and `llm` (`run_pipeline.py:58`) with `--skip-keywords` and `-n`, the `llm` Dockerfile target
   (`:138-157`) and the `nlp-llm` compose service (`docker-compose.yaml:96-102`), and `yake`/`keybert`/
   `sentence-transformers` from `requirements.txt:7-9`.
2. **Service**: `kw_method` and `num_keywords` leave `/enrich`, `/enrich_text` and `/jobs`; the response loses
   `keywords` and `method_used` (`service/api.py:181,629`); `/info` loses `keyword_methods` (`:833`); the KeyBERT warmup
   leaves `service/enrichment.py:499-514`.
3. **Version**: removing request parameters and response fields is a breaking spec change, so under #32's rule the
   release is **1.0.0**. To spare AMČR's activities: **0.23.0** marks the parameters and fields `deprecated` in the
   spec (not breaking) and says so in its notes; **1.0.0** removes them when keyword-extractor's first release is out.
4. **Keeps**: `api_1_manifest.sh` … `api_4_stats.sh`, `api_util/call_udpipe.py`, `call_nametag.py`, the TEITOK writer
   and projection, `/enrich`, `/enrich_text`, `/rescale`, `/project_record`, `/jobs`; the flexiconv route stays a
   developer route (off the AMČR chain, nlp-enrich#10); `annotation/` stays (custom NER, #18, deferred, is served
   through NameTag).
5. **Result**: a CPU-only image; the licence of its output is unchanged (UDPipe and NameTag models, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
