## 🎯 Goal
The issue, in full:
1. *"Both statistical keyword extraction with KER or KeyBERT, and the LLM-based controlled vocabulary terms extraction
   must be separated into another repo `ufal/atrium-keyword-extractor`."*
2. *"LLM-enrich repo functionality related to keyword extraction should be moved to the new repo."*
3. *"NLP-enrich repo should cover only LINDAT service calls for textual inputs."*

Read against the pipeline: **one stage for keywords** — statistical (KeyBERT by default, YAKE selectable; KER kept) and
controlled (the LLM over the AMČR and TEATER vocabularies) — and **one stage for morphology, named entities and
TEITOK**, which is what UDPipe and NameTag on LINDAT produce.

## ✅ What's settled
- **Both keyword kinds leave nlp-enrich** and meet llm-enrich's keyword code in `ufal/atrium-keyword-extractor`, with
  the service `/extract_keywords` (report §2.2 after the meeting).
- **What the statistical keywords must be** (report §5, item 12; #73): KeyBERT by default, YAKE selectable, method and
  score with each keyword, kept apart from the controlled ones.
- **nlp-enrich keeps** UDPipe and NameTag on LINDAT, and what is built from their output (TEITOK, the projection).
- **The LLM stage leaves nlp-enrich** (report item 5, request 7) — now by moving, not by deleting one copy.
